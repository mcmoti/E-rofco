About Society

M-ROFCO is a cooperative society committed to transforming agricultural management for local smallholder farmers. Our digital platform facilitates seamless farm profiling, live harvest weighbridge ledger tracking, tractor dispatch requests, micro-credit loan advances, and transparent shareholding account management directly integrated with cloud registries.
