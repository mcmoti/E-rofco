# E-ROFCO Credit Committee Role Guide

The **Credit Committee** is a vital oversight and management role within the M-ROFCO Cooperative system. Committee members are responsible for evaluating large loan requests, approving sensitive administrative requests, and overseeing the general financial health of the cooperative.

This document provides a clear, understandable breakdown of the key functionalities available to the Credit Committee.

---

## 1. Loan Application Review & Overrides

When an Intake Agent or Field Assessor processes a farmer's loan application, the system automatically calculates the maximum safe loan amount (the **LTV Cap**). This is usually capped at 50% of the expected harvest value.

If a farmer requests a loan amount **greater** than their calculated LTV Cap, the application is automatically flagged and sent to the Credit Committee.

**Your Options as a Committee Member:**
- **Approve (Standard Limit):** You can approve the loan but restrict the amount to the safe, system-calculated maximum cap.
- **Override & Approve Full Amount:** You can choose to override the system warnings and approve the full requested amount based on your assessment of the farmer's history and mitigating factors.
- **Reject Application:** You can decline the application entirely if it poses too much risk.

> [!TIP]
> You must provide a **Committee Justification / Notes** before overriding a system cap, ensuring a clear audit trail for high-risk decisions.

---

## 2. Staff Password Management

For security purposes, regular staff members (like Intake Agents or Field Assessors) cannot change their passwords directly. They must submit a password change request.

**Your Role:**
- Navigate to the **Password Requests** dashboard.
- Review pending requests from staff members who need their passwords updated.
- You can either **Approve** or **Reject** these requests. Approving a request instantly updates the staff member's password to their new requested password.

---

## 3. Account Creation

The Credit Committee is responsible for managing the cooperative's workforce access.

**Your Role:**
- Use the **Create Staff** functionality to generate new accounts for Intake Agents, Assessors, or other Committee members.
- You assign them to specific regional branches and designate their system roles.
- The system automatically provisions new accounts with the default secure password: `Mrofco2026`.

---

## 4. Monitoring Loan Repayments

The Credit Committee dashboard provides a real-time ledger of incoming loan repayments.

**Your Role:**
- Review the **Recent Loan Repayments** table on your dashboard to monitor cash flow.
- Ensure that payments logged by Intake Agents are accurately reflecting against the appropriate Loan IDs.
- You have visibility into the total Cooperative Funds available, allowing you to gauge whether to approve or reject new bulk loans based on current liquidity.

---

## 5. Fund Top-Up Requests

If the cooperative's central system fund is running low on available balance to disburse new loans, the Credit Committee can request additional liquidity.

**Your Role:**
- Use the **Request Fund Top-up** feature to request a specific amount of funds to be transferred or allocated to the cooperative's operating balance.
- These requests are routed to the System Administrator for final processing and ledger reconciliation.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Always cross-reference the `Available Cooperative Funds` balance on your dashboard before making a top-up request.
